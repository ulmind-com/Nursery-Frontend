# Compact premium product purchase panel

## Goal
Make the right-side product purchase area significantly smaller, tighter, and more premium, following the compact proportions of the two supplied references without copying their branding or content.

## Changes
- Narrow the desktop purchase column and give slightly more width to the image gallery, while keeping the page balanced at tablet and mobile sizes.
- Reduce the right panel’s outer padding, heading size, section gaps, and divider spacing so the purchase flow fits higher on the page.
- Make plant-size choices shorter and more refined instead of the current tall tiles.
- Compress planter choices into smaller tiles with reduced icon, label, price, and gap sizes; preserve all dynamic variant selection and disabled states.
- Tighten the SKU, pot size, color swatches, price, gift option, quantity selector, and purchase-button area to match the references’ compact rhythm.
- Keep Add to cart and Buy it now clear and premium, but reduce their height and surrounding whitespace.
- Reduce the visual weight of offers, guarantee, support, and delivery blocks; keep every value sourced from the backend settings and active coupons.
- Keep the lower description, care, shipping, and plant-facts sections unchanged except where alignment is needed after narrowing the purchase column.

## Dynamic behavior
- Live products will continue using backend variants, prices, MRP, stock, SKU, colors, planters, coupons, delivery, guarantee, and support data.
- Preview products remain non-purchasable and continue demonstrating the compact layout only.
- Size, planter, color, quantity, cart, and Buy it now behavior will remain unchanged.

## Validation
- Compare the finished desktop proportions against both supplied reference pages.
- Check 1280px, tablet, and 375px mobile layouts for overflow, clipped text, and comfortable touch targets.
- Verify all selectors and purchase actions still work and confirm there are no browser console errors.
